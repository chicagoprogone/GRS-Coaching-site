Why GRS Coaching?

Real-world experience, genuine passion.

What makes GRS Career Coaching unique is the experience Glenn brings to every coaching relationship. Glenn combines real-world business experience with a genuine passion for helping people succeed. He understands the importance of identifying individual strengths, building confidence, developing leadership capabilities, and creating a clear path forward.

Glenn’s career history was marked by his successful reinvention, shifting industries from leadership roles in Advertising and Marketing to Healthcare, which showcased his ability to understand what it takes for individuals to grow, overcome challenges, recognize their potential, and achieve meaningful results.

Our coaching philosophy
Coaching is about creating clarity, building confidence, and turning potential into progress.

It is a collaborative process that challenges individuals to look beyond where they are today, recognize their strengths and possibilities, and take ownership of their future. Through thoughtful discovery, strategic direction and purposeful action, individuals can create meaningful and sustainable growth.

Coaching is an active partnership. Each session builds on the last, with defined priorities, practical assignments and clear next steps between meetings.

There is no one-size-fits-all program or predetermined number of sessions. Clients engage on an hourly basis, with the frequency and duration of coaching tailored to their goals and circumstances.
