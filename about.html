About Glenn

Meet Glenn Sherling.

Business Consultant  |  Career Coach  |  Strategic Business Leader

"Meaningful growth begins with understanding where you are, recognizing what is possible, and developing a clear strategy to get there."

Glenn Sherling helps organizations unlock growth, improve performance and navigate change, and helps individuals achieve their full potential.

Drawing on extensive experience in business leadership, operations, marketing and strategic planning, Glenn brings a practical, results-oriented approach to every engagement.

Career coaching

Through GRS Career Consulting, Glenn partners with executives, professionals and emerging leaders to navigate career transitions, clarify professional direction, and develop strategies for long-term career success. His coaching combines strategic thinking, personal insight and actionable guidance to help clients recognize their strengths, communicate their value, overcome obstacles, and move forward with confidence.

He is particularly passionate about helping individuals redefine their professional identity and identify new opportunities for growth and fulfillment.

Business consulting

Through his business consulting and Metamorphosis Marketing experience, Glenn works with organizations to identify opportunities, strengthen business strategy, and translate ideas into measurable results. He brings a unique ability to connect the broader business vision with the practical execution required to achieve sustainable results.

Throughout his career, Glenn has been recognized as a leader who builds, develops and empowers high-performing teams. What sets Glenn apart
