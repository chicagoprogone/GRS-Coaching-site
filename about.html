About Glenn

Meet Glenn Sherling.

Executive & Career Coach  | , Leadership Development  | , Business Leader

"Meaningful growth begins with understanding where you are, recognizing what is possible, and developing a clear strategy to get there."

Glenn Sherling brings more than three decades of business leadership, strategic management, marketing, operations, and organizational development experience to his work as Founder of GRS Career Coaching. His career has been defined by building businesses, developing people, leading teams, and helping individuals achieve their full potential.

Throughout his career, Glenn has held senior leadership positions across advertising, marketing, consumer brands, and healthcare services. His professional experience includes leadership roles with Campbell Mithun, BBDO Minneapolis, MARS Advertising, OGI Eyewear, and Unifeye Vision Partners. These roles have provided him with a broad understanding of business strategy, organizational leadership, team development, operational performance, and the challenges professionals face as they navigate their careers.

A consistent theme throughout Glenn’s career has been his commitment to developing people and building high-performing teams. He has extensive experience hiring, mentoring, coaching, and empowering individuals at various stages of their professional development. He understands the importance of effective leadership, communication, accountability, confidence, and creating an environment where people can grow and succeed.

Through GRS Career Coaching, Glenn brings this combination of business experience and people-centered leadership to individuals seeking personal growth, career advancement, leadership development, or professional reinvention. His coaching philosophy is built around helping people recognize their strengths, discover their potential, overcome obstacles, build confidence, and establish a clear direction for success.
