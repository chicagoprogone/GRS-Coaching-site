About Glenn

Meet Glenn Sherling.

Business Consultant  |  Career Coach  |  Entrepreneurial Operations & Growth Leader

"Meaningful growth begins with understanding where you are, recognizing what is possible, and developing a clear strategy to get there."

Glenn Sherling is an accomplished business leader, consultant and career coach with more than three decades of experience driving business growth, developing brands, leading organizations, and building high-performing teams.

His career spans consumer products, advertising, media, healthcare and professional services, bringing together a distinctive combination of strategic marketing expertise, operational leadership, P&L management and people development.

Agency & brand leadership

Glenn began his career at Campbell Mithun Advertising, where over 18 years he advanced from Account Executive to Senior Vice President and Management Supervisor. He led major consumer brands representing more than $1 billion in sales, including ConAgra products, Ziploc, Hostess snack products, Hartford Financial Services and General Mills. His responsibilities included strategic direction, client leadership, business development and integrated marketing expertise.

He subsequently held senior leadership positions at BBDO Minneapolis, where he led brand integration and client services for Hormel Foods and Aviva Financial Services.

Operations & growth leadership

As VP of Marketing for MARS Advertising, Glenn led the development and growth of its Minneapolis regional office, with responsibility for business development, client services, recruitment and P&L performance. He later joined AllOver Media as Vice President of Marketing and Operations, helping drive business growth, strengthen sales performance, and expand the company's marketing capabilities.

At OGI Eyewear, Glenn served as Senior Director of Operations and Strategy and subsequently Vice President of Marketing and Operations (COO), responsible for business strategy, operational effectiveness, marketing, sales operations and customer service.

He continued his leadership career with Unifeye Vision Partners, serving first as Director of Marketing and Outreach and subsequently as Director of Operations for Northwest Eye and Minnesota Retina Associates. His responsibilities included strategic and operational leadership across multiple clinical locations, patient access, business development, marketing and operational improvement.

Consulting & coaching

Glenn is President of Metamorphosis Group, a strategic business and marketing consulting practice he established in 2013. Through this practice, he works with organizations to identify growth opportunities, strengthen business performance, develop marketing strategies, and navigate organizational change.

Through GRS Career Coaching, Glenn brings his leadership and mentoring experience to professionals and executives, helping them clarify their career direction, communicate their professional value, navigate career transitions, and pursue new opportunities. His coaching philosophy combines strategic insight, practical guidance and a personalized approach to professional development.

Teaching & education

Glenn's commitment to developing others extends to his six years as an Adjunct Professor at the Minneapolis College of Art and Design, where he developed and taught the Advertising Account Management curriculum.
